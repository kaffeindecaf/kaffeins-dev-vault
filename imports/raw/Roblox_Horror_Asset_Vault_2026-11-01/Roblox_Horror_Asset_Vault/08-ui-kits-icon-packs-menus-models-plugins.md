## Import checklist

- [ ] Open the Roblox Creator Store asset page and check Get / availability.

- [ ] Test the audio or animation in a published test experience (privacy restrictions can differ).
- [ ] For models/plugins: inspect scripts, dependencies, external requires, and permission prompts before use.
- [ ] Replace `NOT TESTED` with your own test result in the CSV/JSON.
